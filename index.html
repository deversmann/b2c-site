About the Podcast

Between Two Chiefs brings you candid conversations about leadership, technology, and life. Join Francisco Ramirez and Damien Eversmann as they explore the challenges and triumphs of leading in today's fast-paced world. From corporate strategy to personal growth, no topic is off limits. Each episode offers practical insights, real stories, and lessons learned from years of experience at the helm.
